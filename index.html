1. Vocabulary

Learn these words before you read the article.

phishing sites n.
fake websites that pretend to be real in order to steal users’ information
網路釣魚網站；假冒真實網站以竊取使用者資訊
scareware sites n.
websites that use frightening warnings to make users take action quickly
恐嚇式網站；利用嚇人的警告迫使使用者迅速採取行動
typosquatting sites n.
websites with addresses very similar to real ones, often with a small spelling change
網址誤植詐騙網站；使用與真實網址極相似、僅有小幅拼字差異的網站
drive-by download sites n.
websites that may download harmful software when users visit or click something
瀏覽即下載網站；可能在使用者造訪或點擊時下載有害軟體的網站
suspicious adj.
making you think that something may be wrong, unsafe, or dishonest
可疑的；讓人覺得可能有問題、不安全或不誠實的
harmful adj.
causing damage, injury, or other problems
有害的；會造成損害、傷害或其他問題的
urgent adj.
needing immediate attention or action
緊急的；需要立刻注意或處理的
take action phrase
to do something in order to deal with a situation or problem
採取行動；為了處理某個情況或問題而做出行動
reduce the risk phrase
to make the chance of danger or a problem smaller
降低風險；減少危險或問題發生的可能性
unexpected adj.
not expected or not planned
未預期的；出乎意料的

3. Reading

Read the English text on the left. Use the Chinese translation on the right when you need help.

Introduction
The internet is useful, but not every website is safe. Some websites are designed to steal personal information, scare users, or install harmful software. Knowing the common types of dangerous websites can help you stay safe online.
中文翻譯
網路非常實用，但並不是每個網站都安全。有些網站是為了竊取個人資料、恐嚇使用者，或安裝有害軟體而設計的。了解常見的危險網站類型，可以幫助你在網路上保護自己的安全。
Common Types of Dangerous Websites
  • Phishing sites pretend to be real websites, such as a bank, school, or online store. They try to get users to enter passwords, account information, or other personal information.
  • Scareware sites show scary or urgent messages. They may say that your computer has a virus or that you must take action immediately.
  • Typosquatting sites use web addresses that look very similar to real ones. They often change, add, or remove one or two letters in the URL, hoping that users will not notice the difference.
  • Drive-by download sites may try to download harmful software onto your device when you visit the website or click something on the page.
中文翻譯
  • 網路釣魚網站會假裝成真正的網站，例如銀行、學校或網路商店。它們試圖誘使使用者輸入密碼、帳號資訊或其他個人資料。
  • 恐嚇式網站會顯示令人害怕或緊急的訊息。它們可能聲稱你的電腦中毒，或告訴你必須立刻採取行動。
  • 網址仿冒網站會使用看起來和真正網站非常相似的網址。它們通常在 URL 中更改、增加或刪除一兩個字母，希望使用者沒有注意到差異。
  • 自動下載型網站可能會在你造訪網站或點擊頁面上的內容時，試圖將有害軟體下載到你的裝置。
Warning Signs
Before entering personal information on a website, check it carefully. Some warning signs are:
  • a strange or incorrect URL;
  • http:// instead of https://, especially when the website asks for personal information;
  • a scary or urgent message that tries to rush you;
  • an offer that seems too good to be true.
中文翻譯
在網站上輸入個人資料之前，請先仔細檢查。有些警訊包括：
  • 奇怪或不正確的網址；
  • 使用 http:// 而不是 https://，尤其是網站要求你輸入個人資料時；
  • 試圖催促你的恐嚇或緊急訊息；
  • 好得令人難以置信的優惠。
How to Stay Safe
Always check the URL carefully before entering a password or personal information. Avoid clicking unexpected links in emails or messages. Keep your browser and software updated, and use browser safety tools that can warn you about suspicious websites.
中文翻譯
輸入密碼或個人資料之前，一定要仔細檢查網址。避免點擊電子郵件或訊息中突然出現、來路不明的連結。保持瀏覽器和軟體為最新版本，並使用能夠警告你可疑網站的瀏覽器安全工具。
Conclusion
Dangerous websites may still exist, but users can reduce the risk by checking websites carefully and developing safe online habits.
中文翻譯
危險網站可能仍然存在，但使用者可以透過仔細檢查網站並養成安全的上網習慣來降低風險。
